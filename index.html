Find Help Nearby

These are real places around the area that students can use if they need support fast.

    https://www.therapyden.com
  • Psychology Today: Good for finding local counselors
    Find a Therapist
  • Washington 211: Local housing, food, health, and support connections
    WA 211

Crisis Support

If someone is in danger or not feeling safe, these hotlines are active 24/7.

  • National Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: dial 988
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
  • SAMHSA Helpline: 1-800-662-HELP
    SAMHSA Website
  • NAMI HelpLine: 1-800-950-NAMI

Self-Care Tips

Simple stuff that actually helps if you're overwhelmed:

  • Take short walks
  • Drink water (for real, it helps more than people think)
  • Keep your sleep schedule somewhat normal
  • Talk to someone you trust
  • Take breaks when your brain is overloaded
