2. Crisis Support

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger or experiencing a crisis, use these resources:

  • Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988
  • Emergency: Dial 911
  • 24/7 Crisis Line (Snohomish County): 1-800-584-3578

3. Self-Care Tips

  • Take 10–15 minutes to breathe or stretch
  • Limit screen time before bed
  • Stay hydrated and eat consistently
  • Talk to someone you trust
  • Give yourself permission to rest

4. Find Help Nearby

Local mental health and wellness support near Edmonds and Lynnwood:

  • Compass Health — Lynnwood
  • Swedish Behavioral Health — Edmonds
  • Catholic Community Services
  • Mindful Therapy Group — Mountlake Terrace

5. Plan Your Next Visit

Use this checklist to plan your next counseling or wellness appointment:

  • Choose in-person or virtual session
  • Write down what you want to talk about
  • Bring any needed documents or notes
  • Arrive 10 minutes early if on campus
